New to websites? Four words, explained.

You'll see these in the plans above.

Domain
Your web address, like yourbusiness.in. It's what people type to find you.
Hosting
The space that keeps your website online so people can visit it anytime.
Static
A site with fixed content that stays as designed until you ask us to change it.
Dynamic
A site that can show content that changes, rather than only fixed pages.

Common questions

Still unsure? Message us and we'll answer on WhatsApp.

Which plan should I pick?

Use the "Tick what you need" box in the plans section and it will suggest one. Or message us on WhatsApp about your business and we'll recommend a plan.

Do I need my own domain and hosting?

To be online, a site needs both. Starter doesn't include them. Business and every plan above it include a domain and hosting.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every plan has a mobile-friendly layout, so your site looks right on phones, tablets and computers.

How long does it take?

Most sites go live in about a week. When you message us, we'll confirm the timeline for your project.

What if I need something that isn't in a plan?

Tell us what you need, such as extra pages, advanced features or ongoing maintenance, and we'll give you a straight quote.

Who will I be talking to?

A real person on our small team. There's no call centre and no chatbot.
