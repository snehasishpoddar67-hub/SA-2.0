Simple words explained

Domain

Your web address, like yourbusiness.in. Customers type it to find you.

Hosting

The space that keeps your website online 24/7 so people can open it anytime.

Static website

A finished website that stays the same until you ask us to change it. Best for most small businesses.

Dynamic website

A website whose content can be updated easily, without rebuilding the whole site.
